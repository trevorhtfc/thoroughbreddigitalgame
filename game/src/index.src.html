Newmarket · Royal Ascot · one season

ThoroughbredAscot

Build a stable in Newmarket, breed and buy your string, then take them to Royal Ascot and win as much prize money as you can. Win enough and you're crowned Royal Ascot Champion.

  1. The National StudSix mares are dealt to you, two of each grade. Send them to the stallions, buy one more, or send back any you don't want.
  2. TattersallsBid against rival buyers for yearlings out of the mares nobody bred. No going back.
  3. The Jockey ClubEnter one horse per race, bringing on any 2-year-olds who need it.
  4. Royal AscotTen races over five days, watched in 3D. Prize money is its own pot, apart from your budget.
