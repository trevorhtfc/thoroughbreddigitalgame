The American Ring (turf course inside a dirt track) Racing

The American Ring: the Ring's track as a turf course inside a 24 m dirt track, as at Churchill Downs, Santa Anita and Keeneland (the owner's maps), for the Breeders' Cup turf races. The dirt is scenery (no racing on it), with its own outer rail; the chutes cross it. A long white stand side beyond the dirt, an open infield with a results board, a paved plaza and a lake, and rows of stable barns beyond the back straight.

Stands in for. The Breeders' Cup turf races (American turf courses).

The track. About 8.4f round, a 1.4f run-in. Drawn from a few numbers (the corners and bends of the loop, the chutes), so it can be reshaped quickly.

The stands. American racetrack: old small stands and pavilions clustered round the winning post, near the end of the straight, the crowd up to the rail, the festival in the infield opposite.
