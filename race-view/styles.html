Thoroughbred Ascot · style review

Race view styles

Every look, grandstand and tree style the 3D race can wear, side by side. Each picture is the same race at the same moment: on the left the drone along the stand before the off (for the trees, the back straight), on the right the field coming up the straight. Tap a picture to see it bigger, or open the real race in that style. Choose one of each, and the game will ship only those.

1 · Overall look

The whole picture

The look sets the colour, the light and the finish of everything: the turf, the trees, the crowd, the buildings, and the grade over the top. "Storybook" is what the game uses today.

2 · Grandstand

The stand's dress

Only the main stand changes: its colours, its surface and any line work round its edges. Shown here with today's overall look.

3 · Trees

The trees

Leafy is the set the game uses today (owner, 7 Nov: "I actually preferred the old trees"). Clay is the finish study's set.

4 · Mix and match

Try a combination

Any look with any stand and either set of trees, in the real race.
