A · Smoothed 1C

  • Same people and proportions, rebuilt from soft rounded solids.
  • Arms as tapered rolls with round elbows, a rolled cuff and a mitten hand.
  • The tie is gone: a short soft V of waistcoat pressed onto the chest, and a rolled collar round the neck.
  • Rounded shoes, hems that roll under, round-edged brims.

About 1,700–2,200 triangles near the camera (five or six times 1C), with a far version of 500–750 and no arms.
