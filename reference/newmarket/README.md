# Newmarket references (owner, 8 Nov 2026)

For building Newmarket as the game's second course. Private reference only (TV frames and photos are not ours to publish).

- `rowley-mile-plan.jpg`: the Rowley Mile plan. Right-handed; the straight is 1m2f from the post to the bend; the
  Cesarewitch leg runs up to the right to the 2m2f and 2½m starts; 1m4f starts just round the bend.
- `grandstand.jpg`: the Millennium Grandstand. Owner: the stands should be interpretive, and bigger and longer for effect.
- `the-dip-notes.png`: the Dip: downhill from about 2f out (by "The Bushes"), the bottom at the 1f pole, then a sharp
  climb to the post.
- `tv-1m4f-frames.jpg`, `tv-1m4f-finish.jpg`: frames every 15 s and the finish of a 1m4f race the owner recorded
  (Racing TV): open heath, low hedges and tree lines on the horizon, white plastic rails, side-on tracking car,
  head-on long lens down the straight, the ground falling away into the Dip and rising to the line.
- Owner: model the mound / dyke (the Devil's Dyke) on the far side of the track from the camera, as a visual screen.
