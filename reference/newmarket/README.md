# Newmarket references (owner, 8 Nov 2026)

For building Newmarket as the game's second course. Private reference only (TV frames and photos are not ours to publish).

- `rowley-mile-plan.jpg`: the Rowley Mile plan. Right-handed; the straight is 1m2f from the post to the bend; the
  Cesarewitch leg runs up to the right to the 2m2f and 2½m starts; 1m4f starts just round the bend.
- `grandstand.jpg`: the Millennium Grandstand. Owner: the stands should be interpretive, and bigger and longer for effect.
- `the-dip-notes.png`: the Dip: downhill from about 2f out (by "The Bushes"), the bottom at the 1f pole, then a sharp
  climb to the post.
- `tv-1m4f-frames.jpg`, `tv-1m4f-finish.jpg`: frames every 15 s and the finish of a 1m4f race the owner recorded
  (Racing TV): open heath, low hedges and tree lines on the horizon, white plastic rails, side-on tracking car,
  head-on long lens down the straight, the ground falling away into the Dip and rising to the line.
- Owner: model the mound / dyke (the Devil's Dyke) on the far side of the track from the camera, as a visual screen.
- Owner, 8 Nov (second look at the draft): instead of a tall dyke far back, a **gentle bank behind the far rail the
  whole way round**, closer to the track than real life; **tree tops** showing beyond it; **advertising boards**
  dotted along the course (far side only). `plan-draft.png` is now the second version, with a cross-section showing
  what each camera sees over the bank.
- `owner-sketch-zones.png`, `owner-sketch-arrows.png` (8 Nov): the owner's sketch of BOTH Newmarket courses on one map
  (north at the bottom). Purple: each course's grandstands; yellow: festival areas (beside each course's stands, and the
  July Course's far side); orange: the Dyke, in the wedge between the two straights; red: the way they run to each
  finish; blue: each course's main stand camera, looking across its track towards the Dyke. `site-plan-draft.png` is
  the two courses built from it (`tools/course/newmarket/site_plan.py`), with the sketch beside it.

- The stand side's architecture (owner, 8 Nov evening): `aerial-stands-marked.png` (his aerial marked 1 = the Millennium
  Grandstand, 2 = the finish line, 3 = the old stand up the course), `millennium-grandstand.png` (point 1: the wave
  canopies, the glazed stair tower and the lower Rowley stand with its clock cupola and brick end) and
  `old-stand-victorian.png` (point 3: a two-tier Victorian grandstand under a hipped slate roof with dormers, a lantern
  and chimneys, a white judge's kiosk and a small pavilion). Built as `tools/blender/build_rowley_stands.py`; a first
  look in place in `renders/` (`tools/blender/preview_rowley_stands.py`).
