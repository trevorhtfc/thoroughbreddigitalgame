Royal Ascot · five days · ten races

ThoroughbredAscot

Breed and buy a stable, send a contender to every race, and win as much prize money as you can. Win enough and you're crowned Royal Ascot Champion.

  1. BreedStart with £1,750,000. Buy mares at the mares sale (choose one of three each time), then breed them. A mare can be at most one star above her stallion; outcrosses breed best.
  2. TattersallsThen bid against rival buyers for foals out of the mares nobody bred. Once you leave the stud, there's no going back.
  3. Develop and enterFoals arrive as 2-year-olds. Develop one to 3 or 4 to change its rating and trip, then enter one horse per race.
  4. Race weekTen races, two a day, drawn fresh each card: Group races, Listed and big handicaps. Watch each one at Ascot in 3D or sim it. Rating, trip, going and pace decide it. Prize money goes into its own pot, apart from your budget.
